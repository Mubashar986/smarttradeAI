ABSTRACT 
 
The quick growth of digital asset trading has brought millions of traders into the markets, but to be 
successful now, you need more than just manually executing trades—you need automation. Traders 
put in a lot of time and money to make indicators, expert advisors, and backtesting systems, but 
making reliable trading bots, indicators, and backtesting systems is still hard and expensive. Our 
solution changes the game by letting traders easily turn their strategies into Pine Script or MQL4/5, 
deploy fully automated trading bots, and run smart backtest with parameter optimization. This is a 
powerful, all-in-one platform that makes advanced trading automation easy, profitable, and 
available to all traders. 
Our system is using Pine Script in Trading View for strategies. Also, we are removing the gap 
between outputs and Expert Adviser by using Python webhook and signal server in MT4.Basic 
features are position sizing, stop loss/take profit enforcement, drawdown monitoring and recording 
all the trades executed. So, we remove the gap between the real time execution and those conceptual 
models by providing a robust pipeline. This solution proposed by us is solving problems like delayed 
decisions by humans and inability of non-technical traders to test their complicated strategies. 
1. Introduction 
 
During these last years, the continuous expanding nature of the virtual assets (stocks) has attracted 
hundreds of thousands of traders to this market. But due to fast paced conditions of markets 
nowadays doing work manually cannot guarantee your success rather it increases your effort and 
wastes your time. So, now in order to deal with this situation there are automation tools which have 
come in the market which are able to respond quickly to changes or fluctuations in prices leaving 
the inability of human beings to make delayed decisions due to emotional weaknesses. So, AI 
automation has revolutionized the way people are trading these days. 
 
As we discussed above, market prices are fluctuating every time whether it is day or night. So, it is 
impossible for any single person no matter how much expert he/she is, cannot keep up with the 
market trends. That makes with manual trading it is easy to miss opportunities or lose money on 
errors. In order to be ahead of the pack, traders must have the automation software to assist them in 
making smarter decisions and react quicker without having to do it all manually every time. 
 
This movement towards the automation has changed a lot of things. Like how people used to trade 
and how they used to formulate strategies and then execute it by themselves. As people who are 
connected to this trading business know that most of the profits from trading are earned through 
indicators. Yet it is very hard to make good and stable trading bots because it requires a lot of effort 
and cost. Effort in a sense that first a trader who has new strategy, first he has to find a good developer 
with good knowledge of math and business and on his own field which is obviously good grip on 
software development. And cost in a sense that it is a very expensive work as we want to build a 
stable trading strategies which requires extensive back testing. 
2. Problem Statement 
Most traders still employ manual means to execute trades and to backtest their strategies in the fast-
changing universe of digital trading today. Manual trading consumes much time, is error-prone, and 
is often too slow to suit modern markets where prices change so quickly. The complexity of 
programming and the steep development cost also make it difficult for traders to develop reliable 
trading bots, indicators, and backtesting tools. 
 
As a result, even skilled traders struggle to turn their ideas into automated systems that can work

3. Proposed Solution 
Basically, our projects present a fast and robust trading platform which is automated in such a way 
it is very convenient and friendly to use for a new user and this can streamline the whole process to 
address the issue discussed in problem statement. The best part about our solution is that our platform

We will provide traders with guidelines for how to manage risks and how to optimize the parameters

without requiring extensive technical expertise. An additional objective is to provide functionalities 
such as position sizing, 
stop-loss and take-profit levels, and automated trade recording for improved risk management.

Requirement Elicitation & Analysis:  
In this step we will gather requirements from traders, developers and experts in algorithmic trading 
to identify the functional and non-functional requirements for our platform. We will gather 
requirements by join online trading forums and Subreddits. Requirements for features like strategy-

optimized backtesting, multi-exchange support, trading view charting interface integrations and risk

System Design: 
Designing the UI/UX mockups for the web and desktop. Designing backend architecture and 
workflow for whole system, database schema, and data flow for trading signals, market data, and 
bot execution are defined. Integration points with trading APIs (e.g., Meta trader, Trading view) are 
planned to ensure smooth automation. 
Strategy Conversion Module Development: 
This Module will be developed to translate user-defined trading strategies in natural language into 
algorithmic scripting languages like pine script for trading view and mql4/5 for the Meta trader 
bases on the requirement. This module will also have coding chat-bot that can help user for onwards

requirements.  strategy templates are implemented to simplify more for translating strategy into

like order placement, opening and closing trades, stop-loss/limit order management, and real-time 
monitoring and journaling all trades. Both will be optimized for low latency and reliability to ensure 
trades are executed efficiently and effectively according to the traders need. 
Backtesting & Optimization Engine:  
Creating the backtesting module to simulate trading strategies on historical market data and on 
different timeframes. Parameter optimization algorithms are used to identify the best-performing 
parameters or configurations in each backtest by the backtesting engines. Results are visualized 
through interactive charts, performance metrics, and trade logs for informed decision-making.

7.2 System Architecture 
1.Presentation Layer  
•This layer includes chatbot interface where traders can describe and write their strategies in plain 
language.  
•A dashboard displays important information like backtesting results, optimized parameters, and 
performance of live bots. 
•It will also provide interactive trade charts for trade history, signal visualization, and market 
analysis, allowing users to easily track how their strategies behave over time.  
2. Chatbot and Strategy Generation Layer:  
•This layer will understand and translate the user natural language.  
•Here chatbot interprets natural language instructions and converts them into structured commands. 
Based upon those, it will automatically generate strategy code in user required platform formats like 
MQL4/5 for Meta trader, Pine script for Trading View, or Python using Back trader /backtesting.py.  
•This layer also checks its generated code for syntax or logical issues before actual execution. In 
addition, it will also suggest improvements and recommend parameter adjustments based on 
previous results and strategy patterns.  
3. Back testing & Optimization Layer  
This layer runs multiple backtest across different timeframes and historical datasets to evaluate each 
strategy. Basically, what it does that it goes searching for such combinations of parameters which 
can maximize the performance while managing the whole risk and controlling the aim for good 
returns. After the back testing, all the results are summarized and then recorded in metrices and then 
these metrices are sent back to AI module for reinforced learning and to presentation layer. 
4. Trading Execution Layer: 
After the strategy is tested and later optimized then this layer (TEL) is responsible for running this 
strategy in real markets. It also handles live monitoring, error handling and basic risk controls to 
make sure strategies operate reliably during its execution  
5. Market Data Integration Layer 
This layer helps system to get the real-time and historical market data. It is connected to the data 
sources like Trading view and MT4/5 APIs for fetching prices, indicators, and other relevant 
information. Also, in this layer data is cleaned, standardized and stored in a specific and suitable 
7 of 12 
 
format for both backtesting and live trading, Caching mechanism is also used to reduce latency for 
frequently accessed data. 
6. Data & Storage Layer  
Here in this layer different databases will be used like PostgreSQL store structured data and Redis 
is used for caching. A Pinecone vector database is used for RAG (retrieval-augmented generation), 
helps AI to refer back to previous strategies and performance results, metrics to make better 
suggestions over time.
